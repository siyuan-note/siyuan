## Overview

This major release expands SiYuan's AI capabilities and integrations, bringing more powerful database and editing tools, LAN-assisted cloud synchronization, and a smoother experience across platforms.

### AI-powered knowledge management

Semantic search powered by embeddings and reranking helps you find relevant notes more accurately, while the new AI Agent lets you work with your knowledge in natural language.

### Model Context Protocol

MCP support lets external AI assistants and tools interact with your SiYuan knowledge base through a standardized interface, enabling richer automations and integrations.

### More powerful databases and editing

Automatic heading numbering, improved navigation and folding, richer table and database views, more flexible filtering and selection, and smoother search and history workflows further improve the efficiency of everyday knowledge management.

### Faster cloud synchronization over LAN

With LAN peer assistance, devices on the same local network can share data during cloud synchronization, improving transfer efficiency and reducing internet data usage.

### A smoother experience across platforms

From mobile tabs, multi-selection, audio recording, and custom fonts to better export previews, PDF handling, and HarmonyOS support, this release polishes the experience across desktop, tablet, and phone.
